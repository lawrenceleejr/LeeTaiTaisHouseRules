Mahjong is not hard. It's a draw-one, discard-one game with some vocabulary
and a scoring system that sounds scarier than it is. What makes it *feel* hard
is that most of us learned it by being yelled at across a folding table, three
rules at a time, while ma took our allowance.

This curriculum fixes that. Read the chapters in order and you'll know the
tiles, the flow, the calls, the wins, and the money math — the whole game
exactly as it's played at the Lee table.
